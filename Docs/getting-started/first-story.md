# Write your first story

A file can mix all top-level blocks. Names are unique across the project: characters, facts, dialogues,
quests, chapters, stages, channels, events and archives share one namespace.
Indentation defines nesting; `//` introduces a comment. Chinese identifiers and full-width punctuation
are supported. Follow the chapters below to build the lighthouse story one part at a time.

1. [Characters and facts](../language/facts.md)
2. [Dialogue](../language/dialogue.md)
3. [Channels and events](../language/channels.md)
4. [Quests](../language/quests.md)
5. [Chapters and playthroughs](../language/chapters.md)
6. [Archives](../language/archives.md)

The complete original example is [Lighthouse](../../Examples/Lighthouse/灯塔.dna).
The Chinese identifiers and dialogue in these examples are intentional and valid; the explanatory text is English.
